PouchWise

An iPhone app for quitting nicotine pouches without going cold turkey. It scores your dependence, sets a daily allowance and steps it down over a plan of two to six weeks. You log pouches in a tap or from the home screen widget, and see time since your last one and money saved. In English, German and four Nordic languages.

Vicedex

An independent GTA 6 reference built on one rule: a cheat code goes up only after it has been tested on a retail copy, with the date it was checked. It also has a 100% checklist, a painted map of Leonida, release times for every time zone, and a free iPhone companion with a countdown widget.
